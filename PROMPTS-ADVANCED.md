# Advanced AI UGC ads Prompt Workflows

[Back to the main prompt library](README.md) · [Reviral](https://reviral.ai)

This companion guide turns the repository's prompt formula into a controlled production process. It adds planning and evaluation structure; it does not promise deterministic model behavior.

## 1. Establish a baseline

Render the shortest useful version of the idea with one subject, one action chain, one camera intention, and one end state. Save the exact prompt and selected settings before changing anything.

```text
[Vertical creator format] + [product visible early] + [spoken hook] + [verified problem/use] + [simple demonstration] + [supportable proof or caveat] + [accurate next step] + [filming texture and disclosure space].
```

## 2. Build a continuity card

Record only details that must survive across takes:

- subject or product identity anchors;
- wardrobe, material, color, and scale;
- screen direction and camera height;
- environment, time of day, and key light direction;
- opening state, causal action, and final state;
- dialogue, ambience, or editing-only sound notes.

## 3. Choose one reference role

When the current interface accepts references, give each input one job: identity, product geometry, opening composition, ending composition, motion style, or audio. Do not ask one reference to solve every dimension at once.

## 4. Run a one-variable ladder

| Pass | Change | Keep fixed | Question |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Baseline | Everything else | Is the action readable? |
| B | Camera | Subject, action, light | Does the move improve the reveal? |
| C | Pace | Camera and composition | Does motion feel physically credible? |
| D | Light/material | Action and camera | Are surfaces and depth clearer? |
| E | Finish or sound | All visual structure | Does polish support rather than hide the idea? |

## 5. Evaluate the endpoint

Reject a take if the final frame is ambiguous, identity or product geometry drifts, physical cause and effect break, important text is wrong, or a disclosure/permission requirement cannot be repaired safely in post.

## 6. Prepare the edit handoff

Save the selected prompt, model variant, settings, references, seed when available, output file, known defects, exact text assets, audio notes, rights status, and required disclosures. Treat generated video as source footage that still needs editorial judgment.
